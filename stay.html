Stay at New World Apartment Zagreb

A centrally located private apartment in Zagreb's Donji Grad, suitable for city breaks, business travel and longer stays.

Where is the apartment?

New World Apartment is at Šubićeva ulica 6 in Zagreb, Croatia, in the Donji Grad district.

What is included?

One bedroom, a living area with kitchen, a bathroom, Wi-Fi, air conditioning, a smart TV and self check-in.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in begins at 15:00 and check-out is by 10:00.

Can I request a longer stay?

Yes. You can ask about weekly or monthly stays. The final price and availability are confirmed individually.

How do I inquire about availability?

Use the inquiry form on our website or contact us by email or WhatsApp. Submitting an inquiry is not an instant confirmed reservation.

Is parking provided?

No on-site parking is available.
